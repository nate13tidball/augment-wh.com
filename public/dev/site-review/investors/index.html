Investor preview / working thesis

More capacity from
the battery fleet already built.

Existing storage sites face capacity loss, while new grid connections and major equipment can take years.

Augment-wh is building a site-first augmentation business around engineering, controls integration, and repeatable capacity planning.

/ The wedge(01)

Start where owners already have assets.

The first opportunity is restoration or duration expansion at operating utility-scale battery sites.

01 / Identify

Find viable sites

Screen capacity gaps, owner economics, equipment, warranty status, and data access.

02 / Prove

Build the site case

Define added battery scope, controls changes, utility review, installed cost, and operating impact.

03 / Deliver

Execute with partners

Coordinate design, integration, commissioning, and lifecycle work within agreed project responsibilities.

Why this team

Founder experience connects battery behavior with site controls, commissioning, service, and augmentation planning.

/ Business path(03)

Prove the economics
one site at a time.

The initial offer is a site assessment and integration plan. Commercial delivery terms will be set project by project.

  • Entry pointFind a capacity gap with an owner who can sponsor a site decision.
  • First deliverableA feasibility case covering value, integration scope, cost, schedule, and risks.
  • ExpansionRepeatable controls and delivery methods could support more sites and ongoing capacity management.

A capacity guarantee is a possible future commercial structure. No guarantee is offered today.

/ What must be proven(04)

The investment case depends on execution.

  • Owner demandWill owners pay for a study and proceed when the site economics clear?
  • Technical fitCan new equipment integrate across existing controls, warranties, protection, and operating limits?
  • Delivery modelCan partners install and commission additions with predictable cost and outage exposure?
  • RepeatabilityHow much engineering can carry from one site or equipment family to the next?
/ Next proof point(05)

A real site can test buyer demand, integration feasibility, economics, and the path to delivery.
