Privacy

Site analytics

With permission, Vercel Web Analytics counts page views and visitors to improve the site.

Reports include page paths, referrers, countries, and device types. They do not identify individual visitors.

Analytics remains off until allowed. The choice is stored in this browser for 180 days.

Changing the choice to off prevents future analytics requests from this browser after the page reloads.

Contact form

Submitting an inquiry sends the supplied name, email, company, and message to aAugment by email.

The contact form is separate from analytics.
