/ The operating challenge(03)

Installed capacity isn’t always
available capacity.

Identify what limits usable energy before choosing an intervention.

State-of-charge imbalance
Uneven charge levels can limit the energy the system can deliver.
Service outages
Equipment out of service leaves part of the site’s capacity unavailable.
Battery degradation
As batteries age, the energy they can store and deliver declines.

Augmentation addresses the capacity gap. It must be designed around the site’s operating constraints.
