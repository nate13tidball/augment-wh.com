/ A few practical questions(05)

Before we talk.

Is this software or engineering work?

Our current offer is an engineering-led augmentation study. We use purpose-built analysis and workflow tools where they improve the work, but the deliverable is a practical site decision and execution path.

Does this replace our EMS or control the site?

The current engagement is analysis and planning work. It does not issue dispatch commands or replace your energy management system, plant controller, or engineering approval process.

What if our records are incomplete?

That is common. We identify which gaps materially affect feasibility, risk, cost, or schedule and turn them into a focused diligence plan.
